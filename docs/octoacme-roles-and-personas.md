# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Specialists

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Specialists create test plans, execute test cases, validate quality standards, and maintain automated tests. They work with Developers to define test requirements and with Product Managers to confirm acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Create test plans aligned with project plans and delivery milestones
- Design and execute test cases against acceptance criteria
- Validate quality standards and document defects
- Maintain automated unit, integration, and end-to-end tests
- Collaborate with Developers to verify fixes and regression coverage

### Goals
- Prevent quality issues from reaching production
- Improve test coverage and confidence in releases
- Provide timely, actionable quality feedback to the team

### Typical Communication
- Test requirement and defect discussions with Developers
- Acceptance criteria reviews with Product Managers
- Test status and risk updates during standups and release readiness reviews

---

## Stakeholders/Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholders/Sponsors define business value, set project priorities, approve resources, and make key decisions. They meet regularly with Project Managers and Product Managers to review status and decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals, value, and project priorities
- Approve resources, budgets, and key milestones
- Make or escalate strategic project decisions
- Review project status, risks, and outcomes
- Sponsor alignment across affected business groups

### Goals
- Maximize business value and customer impact
- Ensure projects have the resources and support needed to succeed
- Resolve significant risks and dependencies promptly

### Typical Communication
- Regular status and decision reviews with Project Managers and Product Managers
- Milestone approval and prioritization meetings
- Risk and incident escalation updates

---

## Designers/UX Specialists

### Role Summary
Designers/UX Specialists define user experiences, create design mockups, conduct usability testing, and ensure accessibility. They refine requirements with Product Managers and discuss implementation feasibility with Developers.

### Responsibilities
- Define user flows, interaction patterns, and visual designs
- Create and maintain design mockups and specifications
- Conduct usability testing and incorporate findings
- Verify accessibility requirements and inclusive design practices
- Partner with Developers during implementation and design review

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Validate that designs meet user and business needs
- Reduce implementation rework through clear, feasible specifications

### Typical Communication
- Requirement discovery and design reviews with Product Managers
- Feasibility discussions and implementation reviews with Developers
- Usability findings and accessibility updates during planning and release reviews

---

## Infrastructure/DevOps Engineers

### Role Summary
Infrastructure/DevOps Engineers manage deployment pipelines, monitor production environments, ensure scalability, and respond to incidents. They define CI/CD requirements with Developers and provide Project Managers with delivery and operational risk information.

### Responsibilities
- Build, maintain, and secure deployment pipelines
- Monitor production systems, reliability, and capacity
- Plan for scalability, availability, and disaster recovery
- Respond to incidents and coordinate operational remediation
- Identify deployment and operational risks for project planning

### Goals
- Enable reliable, repeatable, and timely deployments
- Maintain resilient, observable, and scalable production services
- Minimize the impact and duration of operational incidents

### Typical Communication
- CI/CD requirement and release readiness discussions with Developers
- Risk, dependency, and incident updates for Project Managers
- Operational status updates during incident reviews and project checkpoints

---

## Security Specialists

### Role Summary
Security Specialists define security requirements, review code and designs, perform vulnerability scanning, and validate compliance. They partner with the development team to implement security standards and contribute security risks to the risk register.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements, standards, and threat mitigations
- Review code, designs, and architecture for security concerns
- Perform vulnerability scanning and coordinate remediation
- Validate compliance with applicable policies and regulations
- Record security risks and recommended mitigations in the risk register

### Goals
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and business risk
- Build security controls into delivery processes and designs
- Maintain compliance and evidence for required standards

### Typical Communication
- Security requirement and review discussions with the development team
- Vulnerability and remediation updates with Developers and Project Managers
- Security risk updates to the project risk register and stakeholders
